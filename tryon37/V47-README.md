# v47 BEST-EFFORT LIVE AR

Focused quality pass:
- Head and body are separated into different rigs.
- Hood + neck follow head pose fully.
- Chest drape follows head only partially to reduce the "stuck to face" look.
- Hood proportions rebuilt to cover more crown and look less spherical.
- Face opening narrowed and raised.
- Neck wrap moved lower and widened.
- Shoulder drape made broader and shorter.
- Material roughness increased for softer fabric response.

This is still a procedural browser mesh, but it is the strongest version of this
code-only approach without a professionally authored Blender garment/rig.
