# "Presentes que ficam": HEYDAY luxury brand film

Second film, separate from the main commercial. 34 s, 60 fps, pt-BR. Master 1080×1920, cut-down 1920×1080.
Compositions `HeydayLuxuryVertical` / `HeydayLuxuryHorizontal`. Code: `src/luxury/`. Beat times: `src/luxury/timeline.ts`. Score: `tools/make_music_luxury.py`.

**Idea.** Most corporate gifts end up forgotten in a drawer. A HEYDAY globe stays on the desk for years, and every time someone shakes it, the brand comes back to mind.

**Look.** Black and champagne gold, one continuous slow camera move, few words in a light serif, gold bokeh, film grain, anamorphic light streaks, vignette. Felt-piano and strings score.
