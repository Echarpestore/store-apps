# "A próxima pode ser a sua marca": HEYDAY brands film

Third film. 32 s, 60 fps, pt-BR. Master 1080×1920, cut-down 1920×1080. Compositions `HeydayBrandsVertical` / `HeydayBrandsHorizontal`. Code: `src/brands/`. Score: `tools/make_music_brands.py` (126 BPM, one brand per bar).

**Idea.** Show the product through the clients: each brand's logo ends up inside a HEYDAY globe. Bright keynote look (#F5F5F7, bold Inter, logos in their original colours and proportions on white plaques).

| Time | Picture | Text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3.2 s | Clean white stage | «Algumas marcas dispensam apresentação.» | Strings + rising piano figure, swell |

Legal line on the end card: «Projetos realizados pela HEYDAY. As marcas exibidas pertencem aos seus respectivos titulares.» Only brands confirmed by HEYDAY are shown; Apple appears as Apple TV+ (the confirmed project).
