What can I personalise?

Every globe is unique: you choose the base colour, the elements inside, the messages, the logos and the effect — snowflakes or coloured glitter. All made to measure.

We reply within 24 business hours of your message.

Do you take projects for campaigns and events?

Yes. Promotional actions, year-end gifts, product launches and corporate events, with special discounts for orders from 50 units.

Is it really made in Brazil?

Yes. Every stage of production is carried out in Brazil. Our globes are 100% Brazilian, made with high-quality materials and artisan care.
